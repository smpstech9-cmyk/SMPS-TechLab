# SMPS Tech Lab — Admin User Guide

This guide is designed for content editors, administrators, and non-technical staff to manage the SMPS Tech Lab website.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Navigate to `/admin/admin.html` on your browser.
2. Enter your credentials:
   - **Username**: `admin`
   - **Password**: `smps2026`
3. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Managing Minds Behind the Mission & Advisory Members

The **Minds Behind Mission** and **Advisory Members** sections power the leadership profiles on `execom.html`.

### Adding a New Member / Advisor:
1. In the sidebar, click **Minds Behind Mission** (or **Advisory Members**).
2. Click **+ Add Execom Member** or **+ Add Strategic Advisor**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Full Name**: e.g., `Dr. Amareshwar Karatagi`
   - **Role**: e.g., `Chief Technical Officer`
   - **Initials (Avatar)**: e.g., `AK`
   - **Member Type**:
     - `Executive Committee (Execom)`
     - `Strategic Advisor`
     - `Team Member (Minds Behind the Mission)`
   - **Member Photograph**: Click **📤 Upload Image** to pick a file from your computer, or **📁 Choose from Library** to select an existing photo.
   - **Professional Biography**: Brief summary of background and contributions.
   - **Key Achievements**: One achievement per line.
   - **LinkedIn & Email**: Optional social links.
4. Click **💾 Save Member**.

---

## 3. Managing Success Stories & Collaborate Page

The **Collaborate & Stories** tab allows you to configure partnership perspectives, process roadmaps, and case study cards.

### Adding / Editing a Success Story:
1. In the sidebar, click **Collaborate & Stories**.
2. Scroll to **Success Stories Management**.
3. Click **+ Add Success Story**.
4. Fill in:
   - **Partner Name / Headline**: e.g. `Bharat Industries — Talent Pipeline`
   - **Partnership Tag**: e.g. `Industry Partnership`, `Academic Partnership`, `Startup Support`
   - **Image**: Upload a photo or select one from the Media Library.
   - **Case Study Description**: What was accomplished.
   - **Result Metric Badge**: e.g. `100% Placement Success`, `40% Higher Placement`
5. Click **💾 Save Collaborate Page Settings**.

---

## 4. Managing the Photo Gallery (Ordered Newest First)

1. In the sidebar, click **Gallery**.
2. Click **+ Add Gallery Item**.
3. Enter title, category (`Summit`, `Workshop`, or `Lab`), description, and cover image.
4. Click **💾 Save Gallery Item**.
5. The newest uploaded items will automatically appear at the top of `gallery.html`.

---

## 5. Media Library Management

The **Media Library** stores and serves all images and video assets in `/assets/uploads/`.

1. In the sidebar, click **Media Library**.
2. **Upload**: Drag and drop images into the top dashed box, or click **+ Select Files to Upload**.
3. **Copy Link**: Click **📋 Copy Link** on any asset to copy its path (`/assets/uploads/...`) directly to your clipboard for use anywhere on the site.
4. **Delete**: Click the red trash icon (**🗑️**) to remove obsolete assets from the server.
